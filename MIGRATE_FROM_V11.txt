MIGRATING FROM V11 TO V12

1. Stop the v11 server with Control + C.
2. Unzip Selly-Bake-House-WebApp-v12.zip.
3. Copy only your private `.env` file from v11 into the v12 folder.
4. Do not overwrite the v12 index.html, scripts.js, server.js, package.json, or assets folder.
5. Do not copy the old data folder during the first visual review unless you specifically need the old local test accounts and orders.
6. In Terminal, enter the v12 folder and run: node server.js
7. Open http://localhost:3000 and complete the checklist in START_HERE.txt.
