Our story

Good things take time—and every crumb should feel worth it.

Selly Bake House is a home-based bakery serving Rockville and the surrounding Maryland community with handcrafted cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and specialty treats. Each order is prepared with care, attention to detail, and a focus on creating desserts that feel personal.

From a box of cookies shared at home to a custom cake at the center of a milestone, the goal is simple: make the moment sweeter and the experience warm from the first message to the final bite.
